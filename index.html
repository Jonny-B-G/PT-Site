About

Personal training that fits around you

I'm the coach behind Good Life Personal Training. I believe getting fit shouldn't mean fighting your schedule or feeling out of place in a gym — it should fit into your life and build you up, one session at a time.

That's why I offer two ways to train: I'll come to you as a mobile personal trainer across Preston, Lostock, Bolton and Bury, or you can train with me at The Horwich House Of Strength if you'd rather work out in a fully equipped gym environment.
